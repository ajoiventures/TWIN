# College Park parking evidence and feasibility work

Reviewed October 28, 2026. Scope: College Park, Orlando, Florida, with the full Main Street corridor and Princeton–Edgewater priority area. This is a source review and execution plan, not a completed parking feasibility study.

## Ticket additions and acceptance

Reuse #15/#16/#17/#21/#25/#28/#29; PM tracks the subtasks below without duplicating the road or parcel pipeline.

- [ ] **P-01 / #25 — Recover study evidence.** GIS analyst obtains the Orlando Main Street final report and original district datasets, plus the 1990 archival study where accessible. Record document revision, observation dates, geographic coverage, source and limitations. If unavailable, record the exact missing artifact and an unsent records-request brief; do not claim its findings.
- [ ] **P-02 / #15/#17 — Build a space-level inventory.** Map on-street spaces and off-street facilities to stable segment/parcel/building IDs. Separate striped count, unstriped estimate, gross capacity and legally usable capacity. Record source/date, public/private/shared status, restrictions, hours, loading, accessible/EV/reserved spaces, entrances and uncertainty. Include Wellesley garage access and level relationships. Do not treat illustrative parked cars as inventory or occupancy.
- [ ] **P-03 / #16 — Verify usable pedestrian access.** Review accessible spaces, ramps, slopes, crossings and continuous walking routes to destinations; identify missing measurements. Distances follow a verified walking network rather than straight-line buffers. Visibility of a lot does not prove public access or ADA compliance.
- [ ] **P-04 / #15/#28 — Measure demand and turnover.** Use dated weekday/weekend and relevant event observations, stated intervals, weather/context, occupancy duration and turnover. Separate residents, customers, employees and loading where evidence allows. Report peak period, uncertainty and unobserved periods. Do not assume all private capacity is publicly available or count repeated visits as unique users.
- [ ] **P-05 / #21/#28 — Compare options.** Frozen current baseline versus signed Complete Street design, construction phases, shared parking with verified agreements, curb/loading allocation, wayfinding and demand management. Report usable supply, walking access, displacement, neighborhood spillover, business access and uncertainty for each scenario. No automatic claim that widening sidewalks removes a stated number of spaces without accepted plans.
- [ ] **P-06 / #14/#28 — Evaluate capital feasibility if justified.** Analyst compares surface/shared/structured alternatives only after demand review. Include acquisition/lease rights, setbacks, ramps/aisles, utilities, drainage, geotechnical/flood constraints, lifecycle maintenance, operations, financing/revenue assumptions and sensitivity. Use current attributable estimates; no reuse of 2009 per-space costs as current prices. Director reviews physical feasibility; VP reviews the evidence for investment. This task authorizes analysis, not purchase or construction.
- [ ] **P-07 / #29 — Optional operations validation.** Begin with authorized recorded occupancy observations, establish space/ROI calibration and a manually labeled holdout, then measure count error, missed/false occupancy, latency and stale-feed handling. A live availability display requires feed access and validation; no individual identity tracking is required.
- [ ] **P-08 / #13/#14 — Independent acceptance and refresh.** QA reconciles manual sample counts, capacity totals, time restrictions and scenario arithmetic. Publish source-linked findings and unresolved evidence, with a refresh date and reproducible inputs. PM retains the full corridor scope; a Princeton-only sample cannot establish district feasibility.

Responsible AI roles: GIS analyst (sources/inventory), transportation analyst (demand/scenarios), civil/accessibility reviewer (geometry/routes), financial analyst (capital alternatives), platform engineer (optional observations). PM owns dependencies; Director owns integration and acceptance; VP reviews value and scope. Actual independent review is required before marking these items complete.
