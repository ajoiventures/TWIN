5. Related parking inventories and signed parking/driveway schedules for the latest Edgewater Drive Complete Street design, with revision dates and construction-phase access/parking plans where available.
6. The 1990 Edgewater Drive enhancement plan parking-study component, if held electronically. UCF's [urban documents catalog](https://guides.ucf.edu/icud/orlando) identifies item 0308 as an archival lead.

Please identify any already-published download links and distinguish superseded from current records.
