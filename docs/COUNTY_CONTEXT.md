## Contract, provenance and safeguards

`python tools/county_context.py --offline` reproduces the packet without agency calls after the bounded acquisition. `validate(root)` verifies snapshot, required artifact receipts and all source/cache/tool dependencies before returning the report. Both build and read also call the City adapter’s full validator, including its receipt and transitive source/cache dependencies; unchanged City packet bytes cannot hide a changed City cache. Build rechecks the City dependency and packet hash before publishing comparisons. API integration can expose `layers` as paginated GeoJSON, `source_register`, `source_decisions`, `field_dictionary`, `first_five_safe_rows`, `stewardship`, `coverage` and `unresolved`; serve `coverage.svg` only after validation. It must not fetch sources per render or turn unknown ROW coverage into an empty accepted layer.

Ignored outputs are under `data/processed/county_context/`: `packet.json`, `references.geojson`, `coverage.svg`, checksum receipt and timestamped rollback bundles. Rebuild refuses modified/unreceipted outputs; a previously accepted source failing on a later build preserves the preceding packet instead of silently replacing its good layer. Original request/cache SHA-256 dependencies and retrieval receipts are retained. Public access failures are cached with actual request time for honest offline reproduction. Neither root/manual layers nor the active pointer are saved. One bounded isolated regression changes only a City cache file and confirms County read/build reject it before any agency request, preserving County packet and active-pointer bytes.

All accepted references carry original publisher/item/API IDs, safe field dictionaries, original dates and nulls, native CRS and units, retrieval dates, geometry/temporal quality and source-use limits. Native County XY is EPSG:2236 US survey feet, exported as EPSG:4326; analysis uses EPSG:32617 metres. Original DATUM codes are retained without interpreting vertical units/elevations. Dataset/item update and retrieval dates are not acquisition or legal effective dates; those remain unknown. License text is preserved; API access does not imply survey warranty, imagery rights or AI-use permission. Source fields exclude owner/contact/free-text notes and staff names.
