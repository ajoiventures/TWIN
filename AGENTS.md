# TWIN working protocol

Read `docs/INDEX.md` and the latest applicable `docs/EXECUTION_STATUS.md` checkpoint, then verify the current files and runtime before relying on historical claims.

For new or materially changed production scripts, follow `docs/protocols/LEARNING_AND_SCRIPT_CREATION.md` and `docs/COLLEGE_PARK_PROTOCOL_PROFILE.md`. Search existing helpers first. Use the script-contract template proportionately; small reversible edits do not require extra paperwork or approval.

Consult `docs/LESSONS_LEARNED.md` for relevant verified lessons. After a consequential repeatable finding, update the durable helper or record that future work uses. Do not claim automatic learning, model retraining, background monitoring or independent self-review.

Preserve the owner's active Rhino/Omniverse sessions and unsaved work. Keep raw/private/restricted source material out of commits. Use the existing ticket scope and five-completed-ticket review cadence; do not count partial implementations as completed tickets.
