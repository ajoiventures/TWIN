It clearly shows substantial paved rear parking on both sides of Edgewater commercial frontages, marked stalls, circulation aisles and access connections. This is qualitative confirmation for the imaged block, not district-wide mapping, a current capacity count or proof of public/shared access. Original image/source metadata remains in the ignored local imagery cache. No publication or texture reuse permission is inferred.

## Required corrections and acceptance

- [ ] **MS-01 / #17, P-02 — Rear parking hardscape.** Trace/obtain supported paved extents using georeferenced, authorized dated reference and parcel/site-plan checks. Separate asphalt, concrete, planted islands, alleys, loading and roof surfaces. Include connected rear lots across parcel boundaries without assuming a single owner or public access. Preserve access widths and verify boundaries against independent control. Do not cover whole parcels with asphalt or infer paving from missing buildings.
- [ ] **MS-02 / #15/#17, P-02/P-05 — Stall and access geometry.** Map visible stripe orientation, aisles, entrances, curb stops, accessible/loading/reserved spaces and obstructions. Reconcile count and usable capacity with dated signs/site plans. Separate observed geometry from estimated obscured stalls. Cars must sit within supported stalls/aisles at consistent scale; parked models are not occupancy measurements.
- [ ] **MS-03 / #16, P-03 — Rear-lot walking connections.** Reconstruct routes from lots to storefront entrances, sidewalk breaks, ramps, crossings, service/loading conflicts and surface changes. Keep unknown slopes/widths explicit; a painted accessible symbol does not establish an accessible route.
- [ ] **MS-04 / #9/#18 — Canopy continuity.** Inventory visible crown extents, trunk/tree-well locations and heights using dated aerial/LiDAR/street references. Distinguish existing trees from proposed planting and inferred specimens. Replace repeated dark card-like crowns with convincing branching, leaf translucency/roughness, silhouette variation and distance-dependent detail. Keep palms where supported or explicitly illustrative; do not substitute palms for the actual broadleaf canopy.
- [ ] **MS-05 / #8/#18 — Human-height Main Street views.** Finish storefront rhythm, glazing/reflections, doors, awnings, signs, recesses, façade materials and roof/parapet edges from dated references. Trees must frame shopfronts while preserving supported sightlines and walking space. Do not invent tenants or detailed branding without evidence.
- [ ] **MS-06 / #13/#18 — Fixed-view acceptance.** Capture sharp plan/oblique rear-parking views and human-height views north/south along Edgewater, plus Princeton corners. Match references by location/direction/date. Verify paving coverage, storefront proportions, canopy continuity, vehicle scale and contact shadows. Review both daytime and evening; tilt-shift is an optional presentation view, not a substitute for sharp inspection.
- [ ] **MS-07 / #13/#14 — Corridor replication.** Record accepted block extent, measured versus illustrative components and open defects; repeat along the entire Main Street program area. A finished pilot does not complete corridor ground surfaces, tree inventory or street views.

Sequence: accepted reference/ground segmentation → rear-lot and access geometry → routes/stalls → calibrated trees and façade detail → actual fixed-view review. Geometry and landscape leads coordinate so resurfacing does not bury trees, float vehicles or block entrances. GIS/reference analyst supplies evidence; civil/scene engineer authors ground; environment artist finishes vegetation/storefronts; independent QA compares actual renders; PM tracks scope and Director accepts integration.
